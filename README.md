# Entenmann's Original Crumb Coffee Cake Clone

<img width="240" alt="A finished coffee cake" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b4c06139-7911-4a08-8c4e-85284ee6e9d9" align='right'/>

This aims to reproduce the OG recipe. Not the NY style, which is an abomination.

Deploy in 8x8 pan.

## Ingredients

### Yeast Mix
 * 1 cup water, lukewarm
 * 1 tbsp instant yeast
 * 1 tbsp sugar
 * 1 tsp salt

### Dough
 * Yeast mix from above
 * 3 cups bread flour
 * 1/3 cup sugar
 * 1 large egg
 * 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Crumb Topping
 * 1 cup flour
 * 1/2 cup dark brown sugar
 * 1 tsp cinnamon
 * pinch of salt
 * 6 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted

### Finish
 * Powdered sugar

## Directions
1. In a small bowl, Mix warm water with yeast, sugar, and salt and let it compile.
1. In a medium bowl, mix flour and sugar.
1. Add eggs, vanilla, yeast mixture and butter, knead until mixed.
1. Cover and let rise 60–90 minutes.
2. For crumbs, combine flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt in a small bowl.
3. Stir in melted butter until large and small crumbs form. Store in fridge until dough is ready.
1. Grease a 8x8 inch pan. Press dough evenly into pan.
1. Heat oven to 350°F.
1. Cover dough generously with crumb topping; press lightly into dough.
1. Bake 30 minutes until golden and toothpick is clean.
1. Cool completely. Dust with powdered sugar before serving.

## Tips/Notes
* Try letting dough rise overnight in the fridge after kneading. Bring to room temperature before pressing into pan.
* Seems to always taste better a day later, when at room temperature.
* The dough should be wet and sticky (~70% hydration, similar to focaccia), not smooth like bread dough.
* You can let the dough rise in the baking pan 30–45 minutes for extra rise.
