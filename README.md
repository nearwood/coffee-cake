# Entenmann's Original Crumb Coffee Cake Clone

This aims to reproduce the OG recipe. Not the NY style, which is objectively trash.

Recipe targets deployment in 8x8 pan.

## Ingredients

### Dough
 * 250 g (2 cups) all-purpose flour
 * 4 g (x tsp) instant yeast
 * 75 g (x cup) sugar
 * 8 g (x tsp) salt
 * 1 large egg
 * 100 g (x cup) milk (or water), lukewarm
 * 90 g (6 Tbsp) unsalted butter, softened/melted
 * 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Crumb Topping
 * 1 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
 * 1/2 cup (200g) light brown sugar
 * 1 tsp cinnamon
 * Pinch of salt
 * 6 tbsp melted butter

### Finish
 * Powdered sugar

## Directions
1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, and salt. Add eggs, milk, vanilla, and butter. Knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
2. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 60–90 minutes.
3. Grease a 8x8 inch pan. Press dough evenly into pan. Let rise again 30–45 minutes until puffy.
4. For crumbs, combine flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt. Stir in melted butter until large and small crumbs form. Put in fridge for 15 mins to cool.
5. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C)
6. Cover dough generously with crumb topping.
6. Bake 30–35 minutes until golden and center reaches about 190–195°F.
7. Cool completely. Dust heavily with powdered sugar before serving.

## Tips/Notes
* Try letting dough rise overnight in the fridge after kneading. Bring to room temperature before pressing into pan.
